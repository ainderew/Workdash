v3 — what makes it sing (call panel only; chat untouched)

  1. The hearth. The panel is lit from below by a breathing firelight gradient (7s cycle) with a hairline of lamplight along the top edge — the call literally glows in the room. Six embers drift up at low opacity on staggered 10–14s loops. All of it sits behind the tiles and dies under prefers-reduced-motion.
  2. The voice is the hero. Idle tiles sit dimmed at 88% brightness; the active speaker's tile lifts 3px, brightens to full, and catches a warm light from above. Attention goes where the sound is — this is state, not decoration.
  3. Lamplight halo. The speaker's avatar wears a slowly rotating sage-to-amber ring (5s), and their nameplate swaps the mic icon for a 3-bar equalizer that dances with speech.
  4. Mantelpiece dock. The control bar floats half-overlapping the tile field on its own shadow, like an object in the room rather than a toolbar strip. Buttons lift 2px with an amber underglow on hover; Leave gets a warm gradient and pressed edge.
  5. Discipline kept. No neon, no glass, no purple-cyan. One material (warm light), three semantic colors (amber action, sage voice, warm-red off), 150–200ms transitions. The wow is the hearth concept committed fully, not effect stacking.
